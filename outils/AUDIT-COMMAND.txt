Copy the list of texts still in English (one per line)
======================================================
1. In the game page press F12 and open the "Console" tab.
2. Paste this command and press Enter:

copy(JSON.parse(document.documentElement.dataset.dcssTextesNonTraduits || "[]").join("\n"))

3. Paste the result (Ctrl+V) into a .txt file: this is the list to send for the next round of corrections.
